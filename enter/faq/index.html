Answers

who made this?

hannah, who grew up on the internet and is still finding pieces of herself on it.

what is this?

a website about growing up online, and an archive that grows every time someone visits. you give your name, move through the portals, and when you leave, the site shows you what it noticed.

what happens to my data?

while you're here, this computer keeps track of what you do: which portals you open, how long you stay, how much you click. none of it leaves this computer while you're here.

when your session ends you see all of it once. the archive keeps a short summary: your name, the date, how long you stayed, how many portals you opened, whether you agreed to the terms, and how your visit ended. the public list shows only your first name, the date, how long you stayed and how you left. everything else is deleted.

does the camera record me?

no. mirror and surveilled use your camera live to draw what's on screen, and each frame is thrown away right after. nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

why can't i go back after i end my session?

because it's over. what's left is the record.

is this a website or an art project?

yes.

where does the net go when we die?

mostly nowhere. old posts still load and old sites get archived. this one is on the WayBack Machine.

can i stay?

for a while. if you stop moving for about a minute and a half, your session ends for you.
