things i've read and returned to while making this.

§ machines & bodies

1739
The Digesting Duck
Jacques de Vaucanson
an 18th-century automaton that seemed to eat and digest grain. the digestion was a trick: the waste was loaded in ahead of time.
1948
Cybernetics: or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine
Norbert Wiener
where the word cybernetics comes from. feedback and control in animals and machines.
1950
Computing Machinery and Intelligence ↗
Alan Turing
the imitation game: can a machine pass as a person in conversation?
1985
A Cyborg Manifesto ↗
Donna Haraway
the cyborg as a way out of clean lines between human and machine, nature and culture.

§ simulation & the real

1968
Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?
Philip K. Dick
androids, empathy tests, and who gets to count as human.
1981
Simulacra and Simulation
Jean Baudrillard
copies without originals, and what happens when the simulation stands in for the real.

§ the net

1995
Life on the Screen: Identity in the Age of the Internet
Sherry Turkle
early research on identity online: who people became in chat rooms and MUDs.
1996
A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace ↗
John Perry Barlow
a 1996 manifesto claiming governments had no power over the internet.
1996
My Boyfriend Came Back from the War ↗
Olia Lialina
early net art. a story told in frames and links that you click apart.
2009
Digital Folklore ↗
Olia Lialina & Dragan Espenschied
the amateur web: homepages, glitter gifs and visitor counters, made before platforms made everything look the same.

§ net artists

2020–
Cyberfeminism Index ↗
Mindy Seu
an index of cyberfeminist art, writing and projects, edited by mindy seu. it keeps growing.
ongoing
las-pinas.com ↗
Chia Amisola
chia amisola's site. her ChiaHouse lives on the board here.
ongoing
mollysoda.exposed ↗
Molly Soda
videos and performances about the online self, like inbox full (2013) and should i send this? (2015). two of her gifs are in this site's image folder.

§ fiction

1965
The Cyberiad
Stanisław Lem
short stories about robot inventors and the machines they build.
1984
Neuromancer
William Gibson
the novel that made the word cyberspace stick.
1998
Serial Experiments Lain
Yoshitoshi ABe / Ryutaro Nakamura
an anime about a girl who disappears into the network. the sound on the homepage comes from it.
